# Sample-data provenance and redistribution

The files in this example are for software-workflow demonstration, not for physiological, clinical, or classifier-performance validation.

- `eeg/S001R03.edf`: PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset v1.0.0, distributed under the [Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). Source: <https://physionet.org/content/eegmmidb/1.0.0/>. Contains information from the EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset by Gerwin Schalk, made available through PhysioNet under ODC-By 1.0 (DOI: [10.13026/C28G6P](https://doi.org/10.13026/C28G6P)).
- `fnirs/neuro_run01.snirf`: SNIRF public-domain sample. Source: <https://github.com/fNIRS/snirf-samples>.
- `questionnaire/demo_questionnaire.json`: deterministic demonstration metadata created for this repository; it is not a validated instrument.

The EEG and fNIRS files come from different public records and do not represent a synchronized multimodal participant. They are bundled solely to exercise the corresponding import and processing paths. No eye-tracking participant data are distributed in this example.
